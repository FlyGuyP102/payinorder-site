How it works

The same short routine, every payday.

  1. 1

    Start with what cleared

    Your real take-home pay, plus any money that isn't already set aside. Not your salary, not pending charges.

  2. 2

    Cover the bills first

    Including part of a bigger bill that's due after your next payday, like rent on the 1st.

  3. 3

    See your spending room

    After essentials and savings, one clear number until your next payday. If it doesn't fit, you see the gap before you spend.

Questions

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You type in your numbers on payday. Nothing to sync, no logins, and your numbers stay with you.

Is the checklist really free?

Yes. You get the one-page checklist by email, plus Payday Notes once a week. Unsubscribe any time with one click.

Do I need Excel for the workbook?

No. It works in Google Sheets (free with a Google account) or Excel. You get both.

I get paid weekly, every two weeks, twice a month or monthly. Will it work?

Yes. The routine is the same for any pay schedule; only the number of days until your next payday changes.

Is this financial advice?

No. Pay In Order provides general budgeting education and tools. Every example uses hypothetical numbers.
