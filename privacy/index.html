Privacy

Last updated October 15, 2026. Plain English, no surprises.

What we collect

  • Your email address and, if you give it, your first name, when you sign up for the free checklist.
  • Where you came from (for example TikTok or Pinterest), so we know which posts are useful.
  • If you click a link in an email to tell us how often you're paid, we save that answer to match our examples to your pay schedule.

We never ask for bank details, account numbers or financial documents. Please don't send them.

What we use it for

To send you the checklist, a short welcome series, and Payday Notes, our weekly email with one practical payday idea. Occasionally an email mentions the paid Pay In Order workbook. We don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

Who handles it

  • Kit (kit.com) stores the email list and sends our emails.
  • Payhip handles purchases of the workbook, and PayPal or Stripe handle payment. We never see your card number.
  • GoatCounter counts visits to this site without cookies and without tracking you across other sites.

The calculator

Numbers you type into the calculator on our home page stay in your browser. They are not saved or sent anywhere.

Unsubscribing and deleting your data

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom; one click and you're off the list. To have your information deleted, email hello@payinorder.com.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@payinorder.com.

Pay In Order provides general budgeting education and tools. It is not financial, tax, legal or investment advice. Examples are hypothetical. Results depend on your income, expenses and decisions.
